<div dir="rtl" align="right">
(ہم جنس پرستی)<br>
<br>
خلقت خدا تبدیل کر کے<br>
مت پکارو غضب الہی<br>
یوم آخر کیسے بچو گے<br>
ہر عضو دے گا جب گواہی
</div>

In last few couple of years, our media and entertainment industry is promoting these false identities and definitions of gender. 
If we don't act now the upcoming generations will assume those self constructed social constructs to be true and the disease will only get worsen and will spread like a plauge.
