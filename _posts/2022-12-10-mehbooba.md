( محبت)<br>
<br>
کچھ کم نہیں یہ عجوبہ<br>
اول بہن آخر محبوبہ
</div>

Its hear wreakening to see young minds falling into the trap of haram relationships (especially in colleges and universities). 
One of the most common trick employeed to start these relationship is to treat the opposite gender as "sister" while in reality they are not. This relaxation of boundaries due to this supposedly fake relation open doors to many other problems. Hence, this should be highly criticized un-appologetically!
