There is no doubt that the material pursuits and worldly possessions are so attractive and lucrative that a person can not resist them by merely 
confirming (by word of mouth) his belief in Allah or The Divine Creator. One has to ascend himself in terms of spirituality to be able to effectively 
reject what consumerism has to offer. The stronger the urge is, the greater the courage will be required to fight it. Also, when a person indulges himself in material pursuits he descends from this spiritual ladder step by step till the point he does not recognizes his creator anymore and starts worshipping his acquired possessions instead.

So the question that a believer must ask himself is:

What are the ways I can ascend himself in terms of spirituality and think beyond these worldly pursuits and material possessions?
